Where this comes from

Grew up in a village in eastern Switzerland. Live in New York now, and I ain't going back.

At twelve the school put me in the lower track and told me I wasn't the kind of student who goes to university. My family filed a complaint. Spent that summer writing an essay arguing my case, and I got the place. Said in it I wanted to do something with English and maths. Finished at ETH Zürich. My great-grandmother left for New York first. Then my grandfather. My father was born here. I moved in June and I'm here full time, because this city's full of people obsessive about their thing, including whoever's making your coffee. Where I grew up, wanting to build your own thing reads as a flaw. Here it's the baseline.
